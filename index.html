Kirmyprivacy-first browser concept
Concept · work in progress

The browser that works for you.

Kirmy is a browser concept built on one idea: the browser should work for you, not for the people trying to follow you around the web. Trackers blocked by default, a fingerprint that doesn't give you away, and search that doesn't log who you are.

Note: Kirmy is a design concept — a sketch of what a browser built around privacy could look like. It is not a downloadable product (yet).

What it does

Tracker blocking

Block all known trackers and third-party cookies, out of the box.

Fingerprint shield

Randomise canvas and audio signatures so sites can't fingerprint your device.

Tor routing

Route private windows through Tor — send a tab through 3 hops with one click.

Decentralised search

Search through search.kirmy without being profiled.

Strict mode

One switch that turns every protection up to maximum.
